PutMind

Privacy Policy · Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

PutMind (“Snap it. Say where. Find it later”) is a local-first app that helps you remember where you put things. This policy describes how PutMind handles information on your device. PutMind does not operate a PutMind cloud account or PutMind cloud database for your memories.

What PutMind stores on your device

  • Photos you capture for Memories
  • Transcripts/text you speak or type
  • App settings (language, voice guidance, reminders, app lock preferences)
  • Encrypted backup files only when you choose to create and save them

Memories stay on your device unless you export a backup yourself.

What PutMind does not do

  • No PutMind user account / sign-in
  • No PutMind cloud sync of Memories
  • No analytics or advertising tracking SDKs in the app
  • No sale of your personal data
  • No upload of your Memory photos to PutMind servers

Permissions and device features

  • Camera — used only when you capture or replace Memory photos.
  • Microphone — used only when you choose voice input for a Memory or voice search.
  • Speech recognition — uses on-device / platform speech capabilities when available.
  • Notifications — used only if you enable Daily Reminder.
  • Biometrics / PIN — optional App Lock; PIN and biometric checks stay on-device.

Backups

You can create an encrypted backup file and choose where to save it (device storage, Files app locations, or your own cloud providers via the system picker). PutMind does not host those backup files. If you lose the backup password, PutMind cannot recover the file.

Purchases

Lifetime unlock is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. PutMind does not receive your full payment card details. Purchase restoration uses the store account on the device.

Children

PutMind is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Do not provide personal information of children if you are not permitted to do so.

Changes

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions about PutMind, contact the publisher through the app store listing for PutMind.
